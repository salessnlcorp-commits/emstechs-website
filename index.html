Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes! Every appointment includes a free diagnostic estimate. We'll tell you exactly what's wrong and what it'll cost before we start any work.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Same-day appointments are often available, especially for diagnostics and maintenance. For major repairs, we typically get you in within 1–2 business days.

Do you service heavy equipment and commercial vehicles?

Absolutely. We specialize in heavy equipment including excavators, cranes, loaders, and diesel trucks. Fleet accounts welcome — contact us for fleet pricing.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. We stand behind every repair with our satisfaction guarantee. If something isn't right after your service, bring it back and we'll make it right.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in South Florida and serve the surrounding area. Call us to discuss if your location qualifies for mobile or on-site service.

Do you handle diesel engines?

Yes — diesel repair is one of our specialties. From light-duty diesel pickup trucks to heavy commercial diesel equipment, we've got you covered.
